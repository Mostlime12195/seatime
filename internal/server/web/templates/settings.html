Profile

Prompt check

An AI model reads each new prompt you send and decides if it adds something of yours. When this is off, your prompts stay on your computer and your time counts without the check.

The prompts that you send while this is off are checked later, when an application that you connected reads the project. A Hack Club YSWS program's site is one such application. If you plan to connect one, keep this on. Your hours will then be more accurate.

Projects

A hidden project never appears on your public profile. Its time still counts toward your totals.

A project that is not a public repo shows under the name of its folder until you give it another name. SeaTime sends the folder's name, never where the folder is.

If you link a repo, SeaTime can check your commits against GitHub. This works only for public repos.

Prompt text on your computers

SeaTime keeps the text of your prompts on your computers, for the prompt check. You can delete it. SeaTime keeps a hash of each prompt, so your hours do not change, and a prompt that you send again still counts as a repeat.

Prompts that still wait for the check cannot be checked without their text, so their time will not count. If an application that you connected needs your prompts checked later, the deleted ones count as not passing.
